## 👋 Who?

Tech veteran with 17+ years in the trade and 10+ at [Mindera](https://www.mindera.com), where I went from Developer to **Mobile Services Lead & AI Coach**. Husband and father of 🧓🧒👶.

I do too many different things to fit in a box, so I started calling myself an **Improver**: whatever is around me, team or tech, small or big, I try to leave it better. Work is almost an excuse to be with people.

> *"Don't show up to prove, show up to improve."* — Simon Sinek
